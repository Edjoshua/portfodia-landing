## Selected Direction — Signal / Workforce Intelligence

### Design Movement
Neo-editorial systems design: a meeting point between independent digital publishing, control-room interfaces, and premium SaaS product storytelling. The reference direction is intentionally dark, modular, technical, and motion-aware, but Portfodia’s content stays human and outcome-led.

### Core Principles
1. **Outcomes before mechanics.** Lead with what people and organizations can achieve, not with feature inventories.
2. **Progressive revelation.** Let the page unfold as a guided journey: identify a goal, explore the system, then choose a path.
3. **Signal over noise.** Use concise copy, index labels, highlighted words, and visual hierarchy instead of dense paragraphs.
4. **Trust through structure.** Grid lines, quiet metadata, visible relationships, and intentional spacing make the platform feel dependable.

### Color Philosophy
The near-black ground creates focus and gives the bright signal color meaning. **Chartreuse Signal (#B9F227)** is the ownable brand color: energetic enough to represent opportunity and progress, but unusual enough to feel infrastructural rather than generic. Warm white text feels more human than pure white. Muted slate, olive-black, and hairline borders provide a calm frame so the signal moments stay scarce and legible.

### Layout Paradigm
An asymmetric, sectioned scroll rather than a centered marketing stack. The hero uses a split composition with editorial copy on the left and a connected ecosystem visualization on the right. Subsequent sections use wide rails, horizontal overflow cards, bento-like panels, and offset columns. Each section has a tiny index marker and a single task, creating a page that feels like a guided system map.

### Signature Elements
- **Signal index:** small uppercase labels such as `01 / THE OUTCOME` and `PORTFODIA SYSTEM` in a mono font.
- **Connection traces:** fine lime lines and node points that link identities, records, communities, opportunities, and intelligence.
- **Corner brackets + live accents:** cropped framing lines, active pills, and small status indicators around cards.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like selecting a live node in a system, not opening a generic UI. Cards lift minimally, borders brighten, and a signal dot shifts position on hover. Tabs update content in place. CTA clicks use lightweight confirmation to keep this static landing page honest while preserving the feeling of a real next step.

### Animation
Use short, physically intuitive transitions: 180–260ms for hover and tab changes, with a slight ease-out and no dramatic scaling. On entry, section labels and cards fade upward in staggered 40ms increments. The hero network uses a slow, low-contrast pulse and one moving trace; motion pauses for reduced-motion users. Never animate layout properties or rely on large looping effects.

### Typography System
Use **Space Grotesk** for display headlines and navigation, with slightly tightened tracking and occasional italic emphasis. Use **IBM Plex Mono** for labels, metadata, numbers, and eyebrow copy. Headlines are compact and editorial; body text is readable, measured, and never more than 2–3 lines in a feature card. Avoid Inter and generic system-font stacks for the brand layer.

### Brand Essence
**Portfodia is the trusted workforce identity layer for people and organizations building meaningful career journeys — different because identity, proof, community, and opportunity connect in one living system.**

Personality adjectives: **assured, catalytic, connective.**

### Brand Voice
Headlines should sound purposeful and future-facing without hype. CTAs should feel like invitations to choose a direction. Microcopy should be concise, plainspoken, and lightly technical.

Example lines:
- “Choose the outcome. We’ll map the next move.”
- “One identity. Every experience counts.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a compact symbol built from three connected angled bars that form a forward-moving `P` / pathway shape. It should feel legible at favicon scale, with no text baked into the mark. The wordmark is rendered separately in Space Grotesk with slightly increased weight, paired with the symbol as a lockup.

### Signature Brand Color
**Chartreuse Signal — #B9F227.** Use it sparingly for active states, key phrases, system paths, and primary actions so it remains unmistakably Portfodia.

## Style Decisions
- Dark foundation with chartreuse signal accents; no purple gradients.
- Use the dark, technical, editorial inspiration as a structural cue, but keep copy human and outcomes-led.
- Prefer asymmetric layouts, horizontal scroll rails, and in-place tabs over repetitive centered blocks.
- Keep all motion subtle and accessible; preserve a clear static reading order.
